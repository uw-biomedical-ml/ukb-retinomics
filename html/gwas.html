Usage Guide
  • Red line in Manhattan plots are the Bonferroni corrected p-value thresholds
  • Click a heatmap to load Manhattan plots for selected macula location
  • Click a SNP in Manhattan plot to load its heatmaps and zoom on SNP
  • Search for SNPs directly or by gene, select them and see their heatmaps
  • Search by SNP position range, e.g. chr3:1-1000000 displays the first million bases of chromosome 3, counting from the p-arm telomere
  • The dynamic Manhattan plots show the sentinel and 130,000+ related SNPs.
  • The full Manhattan plot of 11 million SNPs is shown at the bottom.
